# Astrocyte exosomes as vehicles for the transmission of neuroinflammation post-COVID-19 infection and post-traumatic brain injury

## Abstract

Many patients develop chronic neurological symptoms following SARS-CoV-2 infection (neurological long COVID), which may reflect a state of heightened and persistent neuroinflammation. Chronic neuroinflammation is also observed in patients with post-concussion syndrome after (repeated incidences of) mild traumatic brain injury (mTBI). The combined impact of COVID-19 (infection) and mTBI (trauma) on neuroinflammation and brain health has not been investigated. Here, we show that patients with a history of COVID-19 and mTBI (COVID-19+mTBI+) have worsened neurological/psychological symptoms and increased biomarkers of neurodegeneration. Astrocyte-derived exosomes (ADEs) isolated from COVID-19-mTBI- patients (controls) did not affect primary astrocytes, but ADEs from COVID-19+mTBI+ patients induced striking astrocyte activation and marked alterations in cellular neurodegeneration signaling pathways. COVID-19+mTBI+ ADEs also caused reactive astrogliosis following intracerebral microinjection in naïve mice. These findings suggest a novel paradigm in which signals responsible for the chronic neuroinflammatory state in COVID-19+mTBI+ patients are stored as cargo in glial exosomes.
